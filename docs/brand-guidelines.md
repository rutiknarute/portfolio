# Rutik Narute — Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Rutik is an AI software engineer for teams that need intelligence to become a dependable product, not a demo. His work connects LLM systems, data platforms, full-stack engineering, and thoughtful interface design.

## Core message

**Make AI useful.**

Supporting messages:

- Build applied AI from source data through shipped interface.
- Turn complex systems into clear, measurable product experiences.
- Pair technical depth with product judgment and visual craft.

## Proof points

- Fine-tuned LLaMA 3.2 3B with QLoRA on 2,658 synthesized instruction pairs.
- Reduced frontend change cycles by 25% through reusable UI/UX architecture.
- Reduced critical bug time-to-resolution by 30% through AI-assisted debugging.
- Built and maintained 20+ reusable enterprise data workflows.

## Voice

- Confident, not inflated.
- Technical, not jargon-heavy.
- Direct, not cold.
- Curious, not gimmicky.
- Evidence-led: prefer outcomes and concrete decisions over buzzwords.

## Visual identity

- Mood: cinematic, precise, experimental, calm.
- Primary background: near-black green, not pure black.
- Foreground: warm mineral white.
- Accent: signal green, reserved for action and proof.
- Typography: Geist Sans for communication; Geist Mono for labels and system detail.
- Imagery: abstract systems, traceable paths, structured depth, no generic stock photography.
- Motion: one focal 3D system per view; responsive micro-interactions elsewhere.

## Accessibility

- Body text contrast must meet WCAG AA 4.5:1.
- Interactive targets are at least 44px.
- Focus rings stay visible.
- Motion becomes static when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled.
